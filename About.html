OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To reduce food waste by building a strong community of donors and volunteers who ensure that surplus food reaches the people who need it most.

Our Vision

A world where no good food is wasted and every person has access to nutritious meals.

OUR STORY

How It All Began

Happy To Help was founded with a simple idea to turn compassion into action. Every day, tons of food are wasted while millions go hungry. We bridge this gap by creating a platform where generosity meets need.

From a small group of passionate volunteers, we have grown into a movement working with communities, organizations and individuals across the country.
